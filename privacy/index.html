Privacy Notice

Operator: BK Corp Club, a South African company. Last updated 21 November 2026.

1. Who is responsible for your information

BK Corp Club is the responsible party for personal information collected through www.bkcorpclub.com, as defined in the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

2. What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Order details: products bought, amounts, payment status and PayFast payment reference. We do not store card numbers.
  • Licence details: MetaTrader 5 account number, broker name, device and session information, and activation history, needed to enforce licence limits.
  • Support and marketing: messages you send us and your marketing preferences.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and site usage, used for security and performance.

3. Why we use it

To create and run your account, process and approve orders, issue and protect licences, send transactional emails, provide support, prevent fraud and misuse, meet legal obligations, and improve the site. Marketing emails are sent only where you have agreed, and you can opt out at any time.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers needed to run the business, under contract: payment processing (PayFast), email delivery, hosting and database services. We do not sell personal information.

5. International transfers

Some service providers process data outside South Africa. Where this happens, we require safeguards consistent with POPIA.

6. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, hashed passwords, licence and session controls, and audit logs. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your MetaTrader 5 account details private.

7. How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as your account is active and for the period needed to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us for access to your information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to processing and withdraw consent for marketing. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

9. Contact our Information Officer

To exercise your rights or ask about privacy, contact us using the details below. We respond to requests within a reasonable time and within the periods required by POPIA.

10. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies and local storage to keep you signed in and secure the site. Analytics or advertising cookies are used only where the site shows the relevant notice.

We may update this notice. The date above shows the latest version.
