Refund & Cancellation Policy

Launch requirement: These policy pages are a working draft. Before live checkout is enabled, the operator must confirm its full legal entity name, registration details, physical/service address, privacy contact and applicable governing-law terms, and obtain appropriate legal review. Nothing on this page claims regulatory approval or legal compliance.

1. Scope

This policy applies to digital Expert Advisor software and related licence access purchased through BK Corp Club. The operator's legal identity and contact details must be completed before live sales. This policy does not remove consumer rights that apply by law.

2. Before you buy

Review the product description, supported platform, licence term, price and currency before paying. Current fixed-term EA options are one month, six months and one year; no lifetime licence is offered. Trading software does not guarantee profits or prevent losses.

3. Failed delivery or access problems

If payment is confirmed but the licence, welcome email or download is not available, sign in and submit a support ticket. Include the order/payment reference and a description of the issue, but never include a password or full card number. The operator will investigate the payment event and licensing record and, where appropriate, restore delivery or provide another remedy required by applicable law.

4. Duplicate or incorrect charges

Report suspected duplicate charges, an incorrect amount or an unrecognized payment through the support portal as soon as possible. The operator will reconcile the payment provider's records and address verified errors in accordance with provider rules and applicable law.

5. Cancellation and digital content

Refund eligibility can depend on your jurisdiction, the type of digital content, whether delivery or access has started, and any consent/acknowledgment captured at checkout. The operator will assess requests individually and preserve any mandatory cancellation, cooling-off or refund rights. This draft does not establish a blanket no-refund rule.

6. What is not a software-delivery refund issue

Trading losses, market movements, broker execution, slippage, spread changes, a customer's trading settings or a decision to stop using a correctly delivered product do not mean the software guarantees a result. However, this section does not override statutory rights or remedies for defective, misdescribed or undelivered digital products.

7. Processing and timing

Where a refund is approved, the operator will use the original payment method where supported and follow the payment provider's processing timelines. Actual settlement timing may depend on the bank, card issuer or payment provider.

8. Requesting help

Sign in and submit a support ticket under Billing / Purchase. The operator should publish verified legal contact details and any market-specific cancellation information before live checkout.
