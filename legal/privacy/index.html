Privacy Notice

Launch requirement: These policy pages are a working draft. Before live checkout is enabled, the operator must confirm its full legal entity name, registration details, physical/service address, privacy contact and applicable governing-law terms, and obtain appropriate legal review. Nothing on this page claims regulatory approval or legal compliance.

1. Scope and controller

This notice describes the intended handling of personal information by the BK Corp Club EA licensing service. The operator's full legal entity, registration details, service address and privacy contact must be confirmed before launch. The notice must be reviewed against South Africa's POPIA and any other privacy laws that apply to the operator and its customers.

2. Information that may be processed

  • Account details such as name, email address, tenant/workspace and authentication-related records.
  • Order, payment-reference, licence term, licence status and activation records.
  • EA/preset download events, support ticket content and administrative audit events.
  • Broker referral click events and basic technical/security logs such as request time, IP address and user agent where collected.

Do not submit passwords, full card details or unrelated sensitive information in support messages.

3. Purposes

Information is used to create and secure accounts, validate licences, deliver software, process and reconcile payments, provide support, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain audit trails, meet legal obligations and operate the service. Where consent is required for an optional use, it should be requested separately.

4. Service providers and disclosures

Information may be processed by the hosting, database, email and payment providers needed to operate the service, and disclosed where required by law or to protect legal rights and platform security. Provider access should be limited to the necessary purpose. The operator should maintain an up-to-date processor list and assess cross-border transfers before launch.

5. Retention and security

Records are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, licence enforcement, support, dispute handling, security and legal/accounting obligations. The service uses access controls and audit records, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Suspected incidents should be handled through a documented response process.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where consent is the basis, or lodge a complaint with the relevant regulator. Requests can be submitted through the customer support portal. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or to resolve disputes.

7. Cookies and analytics

The service may use essential browser storage for authentication, tenant selection and session continuity. Any non-essential analytics or advertising technologies should be disclosed and consented to where required. The operator should confirm the actual production configuration before launch.

8. International processing

Hosting, email or payment systems may process data outside your country. Before live sales, the operator should confirm the providers, transfer safeguards and notices required for each target market.

9. Updates and contact

This draft may be updated as the service and its providers change. Use the customer support portal to submit a privacy request; the operator must publish a verified privacy contact and legal identity before live checkout.
