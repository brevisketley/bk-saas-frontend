# First-Wave Licensing SaaS Prospecting

These are **prospecting signals**, not claims that any company is currently a buyer. They are public examples of software businesses whose public product pages show licensing, activation, subscriptions, or license-key workflows. Validate the current decision-maker and need before outreach.

## Priority signals

1. **Neton Software** — public product flow explicitly includes online purchase, instant licence delivery, email key delivery and desktop activation across African markets. Signal: automated licensing infrastructure is directly relevant. Source: https://www.netongoc.com/products
2. **OwnOutright** — sells native Windows desktop software with lifetime access and licence-key activation. Signal: desktop licence lifecycle and activation controls. Source: https://ownoutright.com/
3. **Hannes Software** — sells desktop products with licence-key workflows and product-specific licensing. Signal: centralised licensing infrastructure and activation management. Source: https://hannes-software.com/pricing/
4. **ProcessModel** — public EULA covers desktop software, subscription and perpetual licensing. Signal: subscription/perpetual lifecycle enforcement. Source: https://www.processmodel.com/end-user-license-agreement/
5. **RepairPlugin** — public terms describe plugin licence keys, subscription renewal, cancellation and expiry enforcement. Signal: automated subscription-to-license state management. Source: https://repairplugin.com/terms
6. **Tax Toggle** — public legal terms describe plugin licence keys, annual subscriptions, renewals and revocation. Signal: subscription licence automation. Source: https://www.taxtoggle.com/legal/
7. **WordPress/plugin developers** — the plugin ecosystem repeatedly uses licence activation, renewal, update access and subscription enforcement. Signal: large horizontal ICP. Source example: https://wordpress.org/plugins/licenzo/
8. **WooCommerce software sellers** — marketplace products explicitly market activation/deactivation, validation, updates and licence subscriptions. Signal: integration-led wedge. Source: https://woocommerce.com/products/software-license/

## Outreach hypothesis

Lead with the operational pain, not a generic SaaS pitch:

> "I noticed your product uses software licensing/activation. We are building a licensing control plane that automates subscription -> licence issuance -> activation limits -> heartbeat validation -> renewal/revocation, so your engineering team does not have to maintain that infrastructure themselves."

Then offer a short technical walkthrough and a no-pressure evaluation.

## Qualification questions

- How are licences generated today?
- Does payment automatically create/renew a licence?
- How do you handle failed payments and refunds?
- How many activations can one customer use?
- Can customers self-service device resets?
- What happens when a subscription is cancelled?
- How much engineering effort is spent maintaining licensing?

## First 7-day acquisition sprint

- Day 1: validate 20 prospects and identify the technical/product owner.
- Day 2: send 10 highly personalised messages.
- Day 3: publish the subscription-to-licence technical demo/article.
- Day 4: send 10 more personalised messages and follow up with Day-2 non-responders.
- Day 5: publish activation-limit/device-binding case study content.
- Day 6: review replies and objections; update landing page FAQ.
- Day 7: run a conversion review and prioritise demos.

Do not bulk-spam. Every contact should have a concrete licensing signal and measurable campaign attribution.
